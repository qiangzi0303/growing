English motion SVG character

Character visual language adapted from Ian Xiaohei Illustrations, by Ian:
https://github.com/helloianneo/ian-xiaohei-illustrations

The Xiaohei silhouette, white dot eyes, thin limbs and restrained white-background
visual treatment follow the repository's character and style references.
This app draws new articulated SVG movement scenes; bundled example PNGs are
used only as style references and are not redistributed in the application.
The gait model and animation controls are implemented in this project.
